1984

by George Orwell

Dystopian Fiction 4.6 stars

Description

Published in 1949, this novel portrays a totalitarian future society under constant surveillance, where independent thought is treated as a crime.

The story follows Winston Smith as he begins to question the ruling Party and its all-seeing leader, Big Brother.
